HIIT Workout for Beginners

High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) is a highly effective way to burn fat and improve cardiovascular fitness. This beginner-friendly HIIT routine requires no equipment.

Warm-Up (5 Minutes)

  • Jog in Place – 1 min
  • Jumping Jacks – 1 min
  • Arm Circles – 1 min
  • High Knees – 2 min

HIIT Routine (20 Minutes)

Perform each exercise for 40 seconds, rest for 20 seconds, repeat the circuit 3 times:

  • Squat Jumps
  • Push-Ups
  • Mountain Climbers
  • Burpees
  • Plank to Shoulder Tap

Cool-Down

  • Stretch Hamstrings and Quads – 1 min each
  • Child’s Pose – 1 min
  • Chest Stretch – 1 min

Tips

  • Maintain proper form throughout.
  • Modify exercises if needed.
  • Hydrate before and after the session.
