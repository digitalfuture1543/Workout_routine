Top 10 Bodyweight Exercises for Beginners

Bodyweight exercises are perfect for beginners because they require no equipment and can be done anywhere. These exercises help you build strength, improve mobility, and burn calories effectively.

1. Squats

Squats target your legs and glutes. Keep your back straight, feet shoulder-width apart, and bend your knees to lower your body as if sitting on a chair. Repeat 12-15 reps.

2. Push-Ups

Push-ups strengthen your chest, shoulders, and triceps. Start in a plank position, lower your chest to the floor, and push back up. Modify by doing knee push-ups if necessary. 10-12 reps.

3. Lunges

Lunges work your legs and glutes. Step forward with one leg, bend both knees to 90 degrees, and return. Alternate legs. 10 reps per leg.

4. Plank

Planks build core stability. Hold a push-up position with your forearms on the ground for 30-45 seconds. Keep your back straight.

5. Glute Bridges

Lie on your back, bend your knees, lift your hips, and squeeze your glutes at the top. Repeat 12-15 reps.

6. Mountain Climbers

Start in a push-up position and alternate bringing knees toward your chest quickly. Great for cardio and core strength. 30-45 seconds.

7. Bicycle Crunches

Target your abs by lying on your back, bringing opposite elbow to knee in a cycling motion. 15 reps per side.

8. Side Plank

Strengthen obliques by lying on one side, lifting hips, and holding 20-30 seconds per side.

9. Tricep Dips

Use a chair to perform dips. Place hands on the edge, lower your body, and push back up. 10-12 reps.

10. Superman Exercise

Lie face down, lift arms and legs off the ground simultaneously, and hold for 5 seconds. Repeat 12 reps.

Tips for Beginners

  • Perform 3 rounds of the exercises, resting 1 minute between rounds.
  • Focus on proper form to avoid injury.
  • Stay consistent and track your progress.
  • Combine with a healthy diet for better results.
