Stretching Routines for Flexibility

Stretching is essential for improving flexibility, reducing injury risk, and aiding recovery. Here’s a comprehensive stretching routine suitable for all fitness levels.

1. Neck Stretch

Gently tilt your head to one side, hold for 20 seconds, and repeat on the other side.

2. Shoulder Stretch

Bring one arm across your chest, pull it close with the opposite arm, hold for 20 seconds per arm.

3. Hamstring Stretch

Sit with one leg extended, reach toward your toes, and hold for 30 seconds per leg.

4. Quad Stretch

Stand on one leg, pull your other foot toward your glutes, hold 30 seconds per leg.

5. Hip Flexor Stretch

Lunge forward and push your hips gently downward, hold 30 seconds per side.

6. Seated Spinal Twist

Sit cross-legged, twist your torso gently to one side, hold 20–30 seconds, then switch sides.

7. Cat-Cow Stretch

On hands and knees, alternate arching and rounding your back for 1 minute to improve spinal flexibility.

Tips for Effective Stretching

  • Perform stretching after warm-up or post-workout.
  • Hold each stretch without bouncing.
  • Breathe deeply and relax during stretches.
