Building Core Strength at Home

A strong core improves posture, stability, and overall fitness. You don’t need a gym to strengthen your core – bodyweight exercises are highly effective.

1. Plank

Hold a plank on forearms for 30–60 seconds. Keep your back straight and core tight.

2. Side Plank

Lie on your side, lift your hips, and hold for 20–40 seconds per side to target obliques.

3. Bicycle Crunches

Lie on your back, alternate elbow-to-knee crunches for 15 reps per side.

4. Leg Raises

Lie flat, lift legs to 90 degrees, and lower slowly without touching the ground. 12–15 reps.

5. Mountain Climbers

Perform 30–45 seconds of mountain climbers for a cardio-core combination.

6. Glute Bridge

Lie on back, lift hips while squeezing glutes, hold 2 seconds, repeat 12–15 reps.

Tips

  • Focus on proper form rather than speed.
  • Perform 3–4 sessions per week.
  • Combine with cardio for better fat loss and definition.
