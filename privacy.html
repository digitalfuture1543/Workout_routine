Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how FitHome collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information you provide is used solely to respond to inquiries, improve our content, and enhance user experience. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

FitHome may use cookies and Google Analytics to monitor traffic, understand user behavior, and improve the website. No personal identification is tracked.

Children’s Privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Users are encouraged to review this page regularly.
